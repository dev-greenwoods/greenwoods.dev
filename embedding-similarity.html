Paste two to twenty same-length finite numeric vectors. Embeddings are never generated here.

similarity matrix & projection

Projection uses cosine similarity to the first two vectors as axes; it is a quick visual aid, not PCA or a semantic guarantee.

What this tool does

Calculate pairwise cosine similarity and inspect a lightweight two-anchor similarity projection for pasted vectors.

Limits

Vectors must have equal dimensions and non-zero magnitude. This tool does not call an embedding provider or claim that a 2D projection preserves all distances.
