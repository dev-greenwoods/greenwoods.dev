How to debug RAG retrieval

Separate chunking problems from representation and ranking questions. The local tools below help inspect data; they do not measure production retrieval quality.

1 Inspect the chunk boundaries

Paste a sample document into the RAG Chunker to compare split strategies, then use the Chunk Overlap Simulator to see repeated text.

2 Compare sample vectors

Use the Embedding Similarity Explorer to calculate cosine similarity on pasted vectors. Similarity alone is not a relevance judgment.

3 Review your test cases

Use the LLM Eval Harness to score known expected outputs. Add representative queries and relevant documents to your actual retrieval evaluation.

Do not paste confidential documents or production embeddings into URLs you plan to share.
