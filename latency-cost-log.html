What this tool does

Aggregate call counts, token totals, estimated cost, average latency, slowest calls, and p95 latency from exported usage logs.

Pricing caveat

Cost estimates use an embedded historical price table (2025-06), not live provider data. Unknown models are excluded from cost totals and identified in the report.
