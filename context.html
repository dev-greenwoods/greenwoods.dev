What Context Planner does

Multi-slot token budgeter. Paste files or text blocks, see total token count, and instantly know what fits in each model's window.

How it works locally

Your input is processed in this browser tab and is not sent to a Greenwoods API. Some tools load their tokenizer or format library while online; the entered content stays on your device.
