PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE

Your prompts are your product. We never see them.

Every Greenwoods tool runs entirely in your browser. Your input is not uploaded, logged, or used for analytics. Do not take our word for it: open your network tab and watch, or read the source. That is the whole privacy policy, because the architecture makes a longer one unnecessary.
